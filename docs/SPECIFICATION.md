# Specification locked to Paul's current draft

## Eight pre-code protocol answers

1. The baseline unit is the consistent county group, c. Disaggregation cells are county × industry, i, at NAICS2/4/6. Aggregation groups counties into states or component economic areas, s.
2. Employment is additive across the prepared industry cells, including reconciliation/residual cells. The frozen support contains 3,058 counties; h = 3 covers 1981–2016 (110,088 county-years). The build stage checks annual employment conservation for 1978–2016.
3. County spending x_ct and instrument z_ct vary at county-year level. Both are repeated unchanged across local industries. Neither is allocated to industry.
4. In aggregation, regional exposure X_st and its instrument Z_st are lag-employment-share averages of county x_ct and z_ct. County and regional endogenous regressors use separate excluded instruments in the augmented county equation.
5. Disaggregation uses county and year fixed effects; the final cell regression adds industry × year effects. No county × year effects are added, since they would absorb county spending and its IV. Aggregation uses county/year effects in county equations and region/year effects in region equations; errors are clustered by region (51 state clusters in the main specification).
6. The invariant disaggregation comparison uses midpoint growth and midpoint employment shares. Its log comparator uses full-county lagged employment shares. Aggregation uses standard growth, lagged employment shares in county regressions, and equal weights in the aggregate regression. No population multiplier is used.
7. County support is fixed. Log growth naturally omits zero-endpoint industries and loses 11 county-years at NAICS6; this loss is reported, not imposed on midpoint regressions. Zero-both-endpoint midpoint cells have zero weight. Aggregation has no county entry or exit on this fixed support.
8. Checks use frozen input hashes, additive employment identities, exact weight/exposure reconstruction, independently saved coefficients/SEs/F-statistics, and the exact stored coefficient-gap decomposition. References are not generated by the new builder/verifier. No specifications are searched to select results.

## Sample exclusion: Kalawao

Kalawao, Hawaii (CCG 15005), is excluded because BEA does not report a separate
income/population denominator for it; its BEA area is combined with Maui. This
exclusion is already present in the original 3,058-county sample and does not
change any reported specification or result. No missing denominator is imputed,
and Kalawao's separately identified procurement is not reassigned to Maui.

The historical income builder incorrectly asserts that Kalawao receives zero
procurement. Its failed assertion is preserved. With Paul's approval on October
27, the replication wrapper treats only this single out-of-sample failure as
an explicit application warning, after verifying that the retained county
identifiers match the independent historical sample. All other audit failures
remain fatal. See [the raw-build record](RAW_REBUILD_2026-10-27.md).

## County spending and instrument

Let D_ct be annual real net military procurement (excluding construction), P_ct population, and E_ct employment. Define b = t − h and the average annual procurement over the h outcome years:

    G_ct = (D_ct + … + D_c,t−h+1) / h
    x_raw,ct = (G_ct − D_c,b) / P_c,b

Only x is winsorized, at pooled county-year 1st/99th percentiles, using the original `wins` function. Outcomes and instruments are not winsorized by this step. A dollar-per-resident change is the underlying unit; the paper reports employment percentage points per $1,000, multiplying coefficients by 100,000.

Historical exposure ends five years before b and averages five years:

    a_ct = sum[D_cu, u=b−9,…,b−5] / sum[P_cu, u=b−9,…,b−5]
    national_shift_t = sum_c(G_ct − D_c,b) / sum_c P_c,b
    z_ct = a_ct × national_shift_t

For h = 3, exposure years are t−12,…,t−8. The national shift uses all retained counties, including the focal county. No leave-one-out or product-composition instrument is substituted. The included builder is extracted unchanged from the existing county-frame routine. `config/county_construction.json` preserves the original h = 2 base configuration; the launcher explicitly selects the already-authorized h = 3 window and observation count, not a new default estimand.

## Disaggregation

County MPGR is 2(E_ct − E_cb)/(E_ct + E_cb); county log growth is log(E_ct/E_cb). At industry level replace E by local industry employment e_cit. Midpoint cell weights are (e_cit + e_cib)/(E_ct + E_cb). Full-county lag shares for log are e_cib/E_cb and are **not renormalized over surviving industries**. The baseline county regression gives each county-year weight one. No fixed population factor is attached to either cell weight.

The endogenous regressor x is instrumented by z throughout. With county/year effects and county-year regressors, cell sufficient statistics reproduce exact cell moments and county-cluster scores. The final industry-year-effect columns use full cell data rather than county sufficient statistics. The original finite-sample conventions are retained: log uses surviving-cell N; midpoint uses the county-year correction N. The decomposition retains its original cluster-influence calculations and the sample component, even though the graph omits the small latter component.

## Aggregation

Set g_ct = (E_ct − E_cb)/E_cb and omega_cst = E_cb/sum[c in s] E_cb. Then:

    X_st = sum_c omega_cst x_ct
    Z_st = sum_c omega_cst z_ct
    g_st = sum_c omega_cst g_ct

The recommended county equation is:

    g_ct = alpha_c + tau_t + beta_b x_ct + delta X_st + error_ct

Estimate by weighted 2SLS using omega and excluded z_ct, Z_st. The regional equation is g_st = alpha_s + tau_t + beta_a X_st + error_st, estimated with equal-weight 2SLS and excluded Z_st. The naive county regression omits X_st; its difference from beta_a is not the exposure coefficient delta. All relevant estimates and their covariance-based gap SEs are re-estimated.

Time-varying weighted county fixed effects do not generally collapse into constant regional effects. The main displayed identity residual is 0.090597, about 0.8365% of the regional coefficient; do not force the county/regional coefficient identity to hold by changing fixed effects.

The exact preserved comparisons are state h = 3 standard/lag shares (main), standard/equal county weights, log/lag shares; state standard/lag shares h = 2 and h = 4; CEA standard/lag shares h = 3. A CEA is a component economic area; 343 appear in the frozen sample. The log comparator is a different outcome transformation, not the same outcome in different units.

## Balance and heterogeneity

For fixed-year standardized county characteristic C_c, the balance diagnostic estimates:

    x_ct C_c = alpha_c + tau_t + rho_C x_ct + pi_C X_st + error_ct

Use lag employment weights, county/year effects, state-clustered errors, and excluded z_ct, Z_st. Test pi_C = 0. Although C_c is fixed (1978 in the figure; 2000 as sensitivity), x_ct C_c varies over time. It is not a regression of a repeated fixed characteristic on panel exposure. Income per resident is winsorized at its cross-county 1st/99th percentiles before standardization. Characteristics are employment, employment per resident, income per resident, manufacturing share, and industry concentration. Population is retained only in the numerical sensitivity archive, not the paper figure.

Heterogeneity adjustments use one 1978 characteristic at a time: employment in levels, manufacturing share, or income per resident. Include county interaction x_ct C_c, regional interaction Q_st = sum_c omega_cst x_ct C_c, and regional mean A_st = sum_c omega_cst C_c. Additional instruments are z_ct C_c and R_st = sum_c omega_cst z_ct C_c. Q is not generally X times A. The existing four-endogenous-variable IV routine and its finite-sample corrections are preserved. No size-quintile interactions are added.

The ordinary first-stage F for each endogenous regressor tests all excluded instruments jointly. These are not Sanderson–Windmeijer conditional diagnostics. Non-rejection of balance cannot establish random treatment or exclude unobserved treatment heterogeneity.
